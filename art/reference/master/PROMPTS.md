# Making more clips of the dog (same character)

Frames from the master clip (`video/desk-dog.webm`, from `davinci__video1...mp4`). Upload one as the **first
frame** (image-to-video), and `07-face-closeup.png` as an extra reference image if the tool allows one.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `01-start-calm.png` | **Best first frame for everything.** Also the loop frame: ask for the clip to end on it. |
| `02-wink.png` | Friendly / thank-you moments |
| `03-paw-wave.png` | Hello, raids, new followers |
| `04-big-grin.png` | Excited: subs, gift bombs |
| `05-looking-at-screen.png` | Reading chat, typing, donations |
| `06-coins-grin.png` | Money alerts (from the cheer clip) |

| `00-contact-sheet.png` | All of them at once, to show a tool or a person |

## Style line (paste at the start of every prompt, word for word)

> Flat 2D cartoon, clean bold dark outlines, the exact same dog as the reference image: tan coat with grey speckled
> grizzle on the shoulders and back, cream chest and muzzle, dark ears, red headband tied on the side, same face and
> markings throughout. Same desk, black monitor on the left, dark chair, keyboard. Solid bright green screen
> background (#1cbc25), still camera, same framing, 24 fps, 5 seconds. Keep fur colours, face and line thickness
> identical in every frame, no new spots, no muzzle colour changes.

## One change per prompt (add after the style line)

- **Raid:** He stands up a little and holds up a white cardboard sign that says WELCOME RAIDERS in black marker,
  wags his tail, then lowers it and sits back like the first frame.
- **Sub (money):** A stack of green dollar bills drops onto the desk; he grabs them, fans them out happily, then
  puts them down and sits like the first frame.
- **Prime sub (Bezo's Bucks):** He pulls a shiny bald cap over his head (headband stays on top), looks proudly at
  the camera, then sits like the first frame.
- **Gift bomb:** Wrapped gift boxes pop up around the desk one after another; he looks around excited.
- **Follow:** He notices the screen, turns to the camera and gives a big friendly wave with his paw.
- **Donation:** A small gold treat jar appears on the desk; he puts a paw on it and grins.
- **Speech bubble (optional):** A plain white rounded speech bubble with a small tail pops up above-right of his
  head, stays empty and open for 3 seconds, then pops away. (Or skip it: the overlay adds its own bubble.)

## Saving credits

- Ask for **5 seconds**. Shorter clips drift less and cost less.
- If the tool has a **last frame** option, give it `01-start-calm.png` too, so he ends where he started.
- Send the take even if the colours are a bit off: they can be matched to the master afterwards, and the
  watermark usually sits on the green and disappears with it.
